This song was basically one giant complaint about my first vocal coach, as my voice seemed to get worse rather than better - partly due to incorrectly thinking I was a bass and training me as such.

Richard used to write 30 second snippets of songs and bring them into our practice sessions and we would listen to them, pick the ones we liked and flesh them out into songs.

From memory this one was called "Maiden-3", and as you can hear, has a bit of an Iron Maiden sound to the guitar work.
