Jon and I first wrote a version of this song when we were about 15. I had been listening to a lot of Goo Goo Dolls at the time and I think this was my poor attempt at trying create something along the lines of Iris. 

We then performed this at a friend's party, with just Jon on an acoustic guitar along with a handful of covers. I often get quite bad post-performance anxiety and I remember afterwards feeling so embarrassed that I failed to hit the G4s at the end of the song and started drinking from a bottle of wine (no glass) and chipped the corner off my tooth. This was part of the inspiration towards the lyrics in Permanent.

When we formed the rest of the band, this was a song we brought to the table - we took a loose acoustic song and turned it into something far more fully fledged.

There's a track on Six Degrees of Inner Turbulence (I forget which) that clearly influenced Jon's guitar solo here, as I remember we were both listening to it a lot at the time.

One of my favourite songs on the EP, and I was lucky enough to perform it at Jon's not-wedding many years later. It was also where the EP name "The Cold Awakening" came from.
